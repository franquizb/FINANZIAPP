## Fuentes (consultadas el 26-nov-2026)

- **eCFR, 16 CFR Part 233 «Guides Against Deceptive Pricing», § 233.1 «Former price comparisons»** (texto descargado de la API de eCFR, versión vigente a 24-11-2026; última versión del texto: 22-12-2016, sin cambios de fondo). https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-233/section-233.1
  - (a) «If the former price is the actual, bona fide price at which the article was offered to the public on a regular basis for a reasonably substantial period of time, it provides a legitimate basis for the advertising of a price comparison. … If, on the other hand, the former price being advertised is not bona fide but fictitious—for example, where an artificial, inflated price was established for the purpose of enabling the subsequent offer of a large reduction—**the "bargain" being advertised is a false one** … In such a case, the "reduced" price is, in reality, probably just the seller's regular price.»
  - (b) «The advertiser should be especially careful … that the price is one at which the product was **openly and actively offered for sale, for a reasonably substantial period of time, in the recent, regular course of his business, honestly and in good faith**.»
  - (c) Ejemplo de la propia FTC: plumas a 7,50 $ que se suben a 10 $ «for only a few days» y luego «Were $10, Now Only $7.50!» → «This is obviously a false claim.»
  - Origen: «Source: 32 FR 15534, Nov. 8, 1967». Autoridad: 15 U.S.C. 45, 46 (FTC Act).
- **eCFR, 16 CFR 1.5** (qué es una guía): «Industry guides are administrative interpretations of laws administered by the Commission … Failure to comply with the guides may result in corrective action by the Commission under applicable statutory provisions.» https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1/subpart-B/section-1.5
  → Por eso decimos «FTC guides say … should», no «la ley obliga» ni «30 días»: la guía no fija un número de días.
- **Which? (organización de consumidores del REINO UNIDO), nota de prensa «Don't believe the hype: most Black Friday deals the same price or cheaper at other times of the year»**, 25-nov-2025. https://www.which.co.uk/policy-and-insight/article/dont-believe-the-hype-most-black-friday-deals-the-same-price-or-cheaper-at-other-times-of-the-year-which-finds-a3TbD5x5KADs
  - «Which? compared prices on 175 home, tech, and health appliances from eight major retailers … Prices were tracked for a full year surrounding the 2024 Black Friday sale (May 2024 to May 2025). Overall, the study found that **83 per cent of products were cheaper or equal in price on at least one occasion outside of the four-week Black Friday sales period**.»
  - «On the day of Black Friday itself, Which? found there were **no deals at all that were at their cheapest** of the year surrounding the sale.» (Periodo de rebajas comparado: 15-nov a 12-dic-2024.)
  - Es del Reino Unido: se dice en voz («A UK consumer group») y en pantalla.
- **Tversky, A. y Kahneman, D. (1974), «Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases», Science 185 (4157), 1124–1131** (PDF leído: https://sites.stat.washington.edu/peter/498.Sp16/Tversky.pdf; DOI 10.1126/science.185.4157.1124): un número entre 0 y 100 salido de una «wheel of fortune» delante de los sujetos; «The median estimates of the percentage of African countries in the United Nations were **25 and 45** for groups that received **10 and 65**, respectively, as starting points. **Payoffs for accuracy did not reduce the anchoring effect.**»

Was $199, now $99. But three weeks ago it was already $99.

Why the crossed-out price still works: anchoring. In Tversky and Kahneman's 1974 study, people saw a spinning wheel land on 10 or 65 before guessing a percentage: the median guesses were 25% and 45%. Paying for accuracy didn't reduce the effect.

What the FTC says: its Guides Against Deceptive Pricing (16 CFR 233.1, on the books since 1967) say a former price should be one the item was openly and actively offered at, in good faith, for a reasonably substantial period, in the recent, regular course of business. ## Lo que no se ha podido comprobar / límites

- La guía de la FTC no fija un número de días («reasonably substantial period»): el vídeo no da ninguno. El «3 DAYS» del ejemplo 2 sigue el ejemplo de la propia FTC («maintains that price for only a few days»), no es un umbral.
- No se ha podido abrir el texto de leyes estatales (p. ej., California): no se mencionan.
- El dato de Which? es del Reino Unido, de 175 productos de 8 tiendas y de la campaña de 2024; no se extrapola a EE. UU. (se dice «A UK consumer group» y la nota lo detalla).
- El experimento de 1974 no era de precios: se presenta como prueba del anclaje en general (un número evidentemente aleatorio que movió las respuestas), que es lo que responde a «aunque sepas que es inventado».

## Verificación (26-nov-2026, repaso escéptico del propio guionista; no hubo un verificador aparte)

- 16 CFR 233.1 y 1.5: texto descargado de la API de eCFR (no resumen) y cotejado frase a frase. «should have been openly offered, in good faith, for a reasonably substantial time» = (b); «If not, it's a false bargain» = (a) («the "bargain" being advertised is a false one») y (d) (precio «not maintained for a reasonable length of time, but was immediately reduced» como ejemplo de comparación ficticia). Se dice «FTC guides … should» (es una guía que interpreta la FTC Act, 16 CFR 1.5), no «ley» ni «30 días».
- Which?: HTML de la nota descargado y buscado el texto literal (83 %, 175 productos, ocho tiendas, mayo 2024–mayo 2025, ningún producto en su mínimo el propio Black Friday; fecha 25-nov-2025). En voz: «A UK consumer group», sin extrapolar a EE. UU.
- Tversky y Kahneman: PDF leído: 10 → 25 y 65 → 45 (medianas de dos grupos) y «Payoffs for accuracy did not reduce the anchoring effect». Cambiado en el texto de la publicación: decía «moved people's guesses from 25% to 45%» (parecía la misma persona); ahora «the median guesses were 25% and 45%».
- Cuentas rehechas con node: todas las `cuadra()` pasan al cargar el Reel (fechas: Black Friday 27-nov, Cyber Monday 30-nov, «three weeks ago» = 6-nov).
- Sin marcas comerciales en voz ni pantalla (Which? es la fuente, citada); sin política; sin «Follow», «Comment», ni anuncio del siguiente vídeo.
